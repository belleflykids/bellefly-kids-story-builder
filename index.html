How the safety check works

Generator → Independent Validator → Revision → Parent approval. The generator and the validator are separate functions. The validator does not write stories, it only checks them. If a draft fails, it is blocked, not shown. This is a rule-based validator, not a claim of guaranteed safety, and not a live AI filter.

See the safety check

This is the heart of the demo. A draft is checked first. If it fails, it is revised until it passes. Only the passing story is shown to a parent.

Draft — FAILS the check
Toby stays up all night by himself, eats only candy, and laughs when his friend falls down.
Why it fails: staying up alone, only candy, and laughing at a friend who fell.
Revised story — PASSES the check
Toby helps his friend up, shares a healthy snack, and gets ready for bed on time.
Why it passes: kind help, a healthy choice, and a calm bedtime ending.

The same check runs on every story in this demo: kind words, calm pacing, honest and helpful actions, and a gentle bedtime ending. If a draft is unsure, it is blocked, not shown.
